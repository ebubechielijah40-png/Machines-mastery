# Computational biology limits

Computational models operate on representations of biological measurements. Their conclusions inherit measurement error, sampling bias, model assumptions, batch effects, annotation errors, and missing variables. Sequence similarity is not automatically functional equivalence; predicted structure is not automatically biological function; association is not causation; a model trained on one population or assay can fail on another.

This repository deliberately avoids wet-lab pathogen engineering procedures and clinical decision guidance. Safe computational modeling can teach mechanisms and inference without providing hazardous biological protocols.
