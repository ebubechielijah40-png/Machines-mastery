# Hints

1–3. Count gene copies, not individuals. Sum m independent zero/one variables;
divide their variance by m² when converting count to frequency.
4–6. H=2P−2P². At eventual absorption P is0/1, but absorption generally takes more
than one step. The chance all m next draws are A is p^m.
7–9. Normalize total reproductive weights. Likelihood ranks parameter predictions
for observed counts; it does not reveal the actual generator with certainty.
Sequencing reads add a second sampling layer, not extra population copies.
10. Set e=r−1/4 in the probability-flow ODE. Its exponential has dimensionless
argument; expected substitutions/site and calendar time differ.
11–13. UPGMA averages every cross-leaf pair. A long pendant B edge makes it appear
far from even its true neighboring A. Test the ultrametric condition on triples.
14. Mutation should make previously absorbing endpoints able to leave. Define
its order relative to selection and sampling rather than inserting a random flip
at an unspecified stage.
