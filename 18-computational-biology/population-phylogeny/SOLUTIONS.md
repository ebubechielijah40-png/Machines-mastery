1.A locus identifies a genomic location/region; an allele is a variant there.
A base is a sequence symbol. Ideal autosomal diploidy gives m20,p5/20=.25.
2.An ordered sequence with j A copies weighs p^j(1−p)^(m−j); C(m,j) choices give
that count. At m4,p.5 probabilities[1,4,6,4,1]/16 sum1.
3.E X'=mp,Var X'=mp(1−p); E P'=p,Var P'=p(1−p)/m.
4.Initial H.5,next expected H.375. Across populations some gain and some lose A;
constant ensemble mean is compatible with mass concentrating at both endpoints.

states bounds nonabsorption by a geometric tail. Thus eventual endpoints have
all mass; E P=p0 means probability of endpoint1 equals p0. Immediate fixation
is p0^m. For m4,p.5 they are.5 and.0625, respectively.
