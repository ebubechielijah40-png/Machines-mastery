# Hints after an attempt

1–2. State which scale and geometry each word describes. Area is proportional to
the *square* of diameter;1 mm=10^-3 m before squaring.

3–8. Differentiate powers of r, not q as if it were constant. At q=1 most terms
simplify. For a fixed force, test curvature of U-Fr. For chain geometry calculate
force per chain before dividing by the cross-sectional area.

9–11. Work is area under a force/displacement graph. Springs in series share force;
springs in parallel share displacement. Net force governs acceleration, not the
internal stress distribution.

12–14. Solve the observation equation for the nuisance parameter. Subtract two
slope equations before substituting numerical values. Check units of the result.

15–17. Center both variables; expand a quadratic in slope. A constraint on intercept
is extra information and can be false. Differentiate log E only as shorthand for
ordinary product/reciprocal derivatives; common calibration errors can correlate.

18–22. Differencing two nearly equal slopes amplifies their errors. Preserve
unphysical fit estimates as model checks. Exact small regression oracles should
use a different arithmetic path from floating code. A valid future protocol is
not an actual observed experiment.
