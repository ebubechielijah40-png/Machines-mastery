# Materials mechanisms and remaining chemistry scope

The developed [energy/elasticity/measurement unit](elastic-evidence/README.md)
teaches a complete bounded chain: declared interatomic energy → derivatives and
stability → ideal geometry → stress/strain → structural compliance → measured
travel → inverse parameter identification. Work its examples and reconstruct the
two-length experiment before treating a modulus as a trustworthy machine input.
Its source map includes selected inspected primary book/institutional sections.

For electronic materials, follow [carrier and transistor instruction](../../hardware-electronics-mastery/03_SEMICONDUCTORS_TRANSISTORS.md).
A mechanical pair potential does not explain electrical bands or chemical kinetics.
For biological context, the [molecular bridge](../../18-computational-biology/01_MOLECULAR_FOUNDATIONS.md)
connects representations to biological assumptions; it does not complete chemistry.

Remaining authoring includes reaction energetics versus kinetics, free energy and
equilibrium, acids/bases/buffers, molecular geometry/solvation, polymer/ceramic/metal
processing, plasticity, fatigue, fracture, creep, corrosion, thermal properties
and real calibrated material measurements. Those concepts require their own
first-principles worked instruction, bounded implementation/experiment where
appropriate, progressive assessment and primary evidence. They are not taught
merely by listing their names here. No full-book or whole-domain completion is
claimed from the new bounded unit.
