# Hints after the attempt

1–3: expand Σ(Xi-Xbar)²; empirical population variance divides by n, while the
unbiased sample estimate divides by n-1. Resampled observations are independent
conditional on the observed empirical distribution.

4–5: label sample positions before enumerating. A quantile indexes a sorted list
under a declared convention; repeated values still have repeated mass.

6–7: when k=0, the empirical model never produces1. For each possible k, weight
whether its interval covers p by the probability of observing that k originally.

8–9: positive affine transforms preserve order. Dependence changes variance even
when every marginal observation has the same distribution.

10–12: a fixed random seed makes a computation repeatable; it does not make the
sample selection independent. Preserve pairs if the target uses within-pair
differences. [Full solutions](SOLUTIONS.md).
