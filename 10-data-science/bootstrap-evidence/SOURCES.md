# Primary-source verification scope

S1: B. Efron, *Bootstrap Methods: Another Look at the Jackknife*, The Annals of
Statistics7(1),1979,pp.1–26, DOI10.1214/aos/1176344552. Inspected2026-10-23 via
[University of Washington-hosted original paper PDF](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/courses/stat527/s13/readings/ann_stat1979.pdf).
Exact scope used: printed pp.2–4, §2 empirical distribution, with-replacement
samples, conditional bootstrap law and direct versus Monte Carlo calculation.
A Helsinki mirror's text extraction exposed only its cover; it was not treated
as full-text inspection. No paper figures, passages, examples or tables are
reproduced, and access is not an inferred redistribution license.

The source establishes historical mechanism context. Our two-value example,
quantile API, variance derivation, Bernoulli coverage calculation, validation
and experiments are original. We do not attribute a universal95% percentile
coverage theorem to Efron or claim to reproduce the paper's experiments. Local
results and tests provide separate implementation evidence. The internal
[probability foundation](../../foundations/statistics/probability-inference/README.md)
teaches the prerequisite distribution/expectation rules.
