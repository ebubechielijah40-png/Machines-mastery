Executed on2026-10-23; interpreter/platform and executed source hashes are in
[evidence/stack.json](evidence/stack.json). The integration suite records one
passing test. It compares vector/density gate routes, expected purity, parity,
full-state overlap and an independently derived logical-error value. Underlying
suites retain eighteen pure-state tests and nine channel/recovery tests.

These are synthetic software calculations. No physical shots, cloud job, compiler
mapping, pulse sequence, device lifetime, advantage benchmark or fault-tolerant
threshold was measured. The exact model, independent oracles and counterexamples
support bounded mathematical claims. They do not establish all quantum hardware
or stack objectives. Memory reconstruction remains a learner assessment.
