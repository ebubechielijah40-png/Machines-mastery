# Independently checkable solutions

These are reference arguments after an attempt. Different implementations and valid evidence designs can earn full credit. A reproduced printed answer without its derivation does not pass reconstruction.

## Causal clocks

**L1.** Generating edges are a→b, b→s, s→r, r→c. Transitive closure adds a→s,a→r,a→c,b→r,b→c,s→c: ten pairs total. Values are a1,b2,s3,r4,c5,u1. Although u1<c5, u has no directed path to c. Different finite trace orderings may list u elsewhere without changing those dependencies.

**L2.** Every local edge increases because that process increments. Every message edge increases because receive exceeds the attached send timestamp. Concatenating such edges preserves strict inequality. A send with timestamp1 followed by a receiver initially0 gets timestamp1 under the broken `max` rule, violating strict inequality. Final local counters contain no send/receive pairing, so their mere monotonicity cannot detect the cross-process equality.

**L3.** Two sends named m violate unique message identity. A send A→B paired with a receive claiming A→C violates matching endpoints. A receive before any matching send violates the model's topological delivery schedule. An undelivered send is valid in a finite prefix. A missing logging record may make the evidence incomplete even when the physical send occurred; a validator cannot determine that from the remaining trace alone. Never silently invent a source event to make a trace pass.

**L4.** No. The available data are compatible with A having already issued `(4,A)` and its message being delayed. An offline sorter sees the complete set; an online process needs additional protocol evidence that no smaller relevant request is missing. Acknowledgments, channel assumptions and membership rules belong to a complete ordering protocol. The timestamp number alone supplies none of them.

**L5.** The graph oracle constructs local-successor and send/receive edges, then traverses reachable nodes from each event. The stamper stores one counter per process and the sent value per message. Neither uses the other's outputs. Their comparison tests a representation against a relation generated by a different mechanism. This independence does not make the oracle infallible: a known hand-constructed closure also tests it.

## Fourier factorization

**F1.** With w=i, direct coefficients are 1+2+3+4=10; 1+2i−3−4i=−2−2i; 1−2+3−4=−2; 1−2i−3+4i=−2+2i. Even transform E=[4,−2]; odd transform O=[6,−2]. Combining E[k]±w^k O[k] gives coefficients at k and k+2, respectively. At k=1, the odd term is −2i, explaining the negative imaginary component. “Positive-sign transform” describes the exponential convention, not the sign of every coefficient.

**F2.** Each of log2(n) stages has n/2 butterflies. Counts are n log2(n)/2 value multiplications and n log2(n) complex additions. For n8: stages3, butterflies12, additions24. For n256: stages8, butterflies1024, additions2048. Direct summation has n² input-value multiplications, here64 and65536. Root construction, permutations, allocation, loop dispatch, complex-operation internals and hardware effects are excluded. A count is not an elapsed-time model with a universal constant.

**F3.** A consistently reversed-sign forward/inverse pair can round-trip perfectly. For x[j]=exp(−2πij/n), the positive-sign definition gives coefficient n at k1 and zeros elsewhere by the finite root sum; negative sign places the peak at n−1 for n>2. Test n8 to avoid the n2 coincidence. An impulse and constant also help, but neither distinguishes sign by itself.

**F4.** The linear coefficient result is `[3,10,8]` because `(1+2z)(3+4z)=3+10z+8z²`. Length-two circular convolution wraps z² to z⁰ and gives `[11,10]`. Pad both arrays to a power of two at least `len(a)+len(b)−1`; here choose4. Transform, multiply coefficientwise, invert and keep the first3 entries. Zero-padding prevents this wrap; it does not create extra sampled information in a signal.

**F5.** Splitting the sum into even and odd indices yields X[k]=E[k]+w_n^k O[k] and X[k+n/2]=E[k]−w_n^k O[k]. Doubling n changes B(n)=n log2(n)/2 to B(2n)=n(log2(n)+1), more than twice B(n). One anomalous timing can come from scheduling, warm caches, interpreter warm-up, root generation, allocation or clock noise. Retain it; repeat with a declared procedure rather than deleting it after seeing the result.

## Task publication

**M1.** Naive totals are red4,blue1; one contribution per task gives red2,blue1. Induction: initially no output is accepted. A completion for an absent task adds one entry. A completion for an already present task changes no entry. Hence the accepted map never contains more than one output per task. With deterministic outputs and every task present, summing winners equals the sequential logical-shard sum. The proof assumes the identity and output validation boundaries are correct.

**M2.** Published `{}` occupies the empty task's logical entry and contributes zero counts. Absence does not prove that zero is the correct output, so reduction must reject an incomplete job. Mutating a caller's dictionary must leave an accepted result unchanged; copy/canonicalize the output at publication and return owned observation snapshots. A local snapshot does not supply durable storage after process loss.

**M3.** True mean=(0+10+10+10)/4=7.5. Mean of means=(0+10)/2=5. Represent each shard by `(sum,count)`, combine by componentwise addition and divide once when total count is nonzero. Addition of these sufficient statistics is associative and commutative under exact arithmetic. Floating rounding and zero-record semantics still need specification.

**M4.** Two attempts can send two notifications even when only one output is accepted. A possible design writes a durable outbox entry with unique logical operation ID in the same database transaction as the published state. A downstream receiver persists that ID with its effect transaction and ignores duplicates. Without an atomic boundary, a crash can occur after an effect but before deduplication is recorded. An in-memory set disappears on restart. This is a design exercise, not a guarantee implemented by `Publication`.

## Analytical privacy

**D1.** The log-density ratio p0(y)/p1(y) is `[|y−1|−|y|]/b`. It equals1/b for y<0, (1−2y)/b for0≤y≤1, and−1/b for y>1. Thus its maximum absolute value is1/b. For both neighboring directions to be bounded by ε, b≥1/ε. This is a statement about densities integrated over measurable events; neither continuous distribution has a nonzero exact-singleton mass.

**D2.** b=2 counts. E|Z|=2 counts and Var(Z)=8 squared counts. Solving exp(−t/2)=0.05 gives t=2 ln20≈5.99146 counts. The scale and standard deviation differ; standard deviation is2√2 counts. An observed error beyond that envelope is possible with probability0.05 and is not by itself evidence of an implementation bug.

**D3.** Add/remove changes one histogram entry by one, so L1 sensitivity1. Replacing a person from one bin to another decreases one entry and increases another, so sensitivity2. Treat the entire output vector's sensitivity rather than assuming all k bins can independently change on one addition. The result depends on one-person contribution limits and the precise adjacency definition.

**D4.** For ideal U in(0,1), return b ln(2U) when U<1/2 and −b ln(2(1−U)) otherwise. At1/2 either branch gives zero; endpoints0 and1 yield logarithms of zero. Real generators use finite states, floating numbers have gaps, and service logs/timing/errors can be observable. A goodness-of-fit test over finitely many samples cannot establish an inequality over every event and every neighboring database. A real implementation needs an implementation-specific privacy argument.

## Evidence

**R1.** 891 seconds is source-reported. Zero violations on the saved input is locally observed. Strict clock increase on every causal path is proved under the event rules. “All networks deliver in this order” is unsupported: the model receives a valid schedule and does not control a real network.

**R2.** Supported: “On the saved Python environment and original seeded n256 input, the saved FFT timing samples were lower than the direct-sum samples.” Unsupported: “We reproduced IBM 7094 performance” or “the implementation is always faster.” Historical comparison needs matching original program, input, units, arithmetic and hardware or a carefully justified emulation/model; those are absent.

**R3.** A valid row could contain source `DG04 §5.3`, reported891s, local target“duplicate publication count correctness,” output`evidence/publication.json`, discrepancy“runtime not measured,” next test“validate storage publication under crash.” Put “not executed” in a measurement field when appropriate. The two targets are deliberately different, so computing a speedup would be meaningless.

**R4.** Many answers are valid. For a sequence aligner, define prefix-score state, transition and traceback invariant; compare short inputs with exhaustive alignment enumeration; use an intentionally missing local-reset term as a negative control. Causal graphs transfer the independent-oracle method, while process-message assumptions do not transfer to biological sequence evidence. A mechanism score alone does not establish homology. A full-credit answer makes this boundary explicit.
