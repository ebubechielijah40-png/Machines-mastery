# Entry gate: scientific pipeline

Know arithmetic means, squared deviations, fractions and variances, with units carried through arithmetic. Review [algebra](../../foundations/mathematics/core/01_ALGEBRA_FUNCTIONS.md) and [scientific reasoning](../../09-scientific-reasoning/SELF_CONTAINED_GUIDE.md). The guide derives the uncertainty equations before using them.

Before implementing, compute the mean of25000,25002,24998 and explain the difference between individual-reading scatter, uncertainty of their mean and a shared calibration correction. Required tools are Python3.12 standard library only. All supplied readings and calibration parameters are synthetic assumptions, not measured instrument evidence.
