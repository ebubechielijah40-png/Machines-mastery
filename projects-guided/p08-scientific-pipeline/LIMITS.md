# Scientific model limits

The measurand is constant temperature under unchanged conditions; reading errors are assumed independent and independent of one shared offset estimate. Calibration standard uncertainty is supplied. The pipeline does not infer these assumptions, a probability coverage interval, drift, outlier validity or physical truth.

Input is a restricted bounded ASCII CSV dialect with integer milli-Celsius, contiguous ordinal sequence and complete rows. There are no timestamps, missing values, general quoted fields, parallel merge or database transactions. Fractions preserve exact summary arithmetic; the final standard deviation uses a floating square root. SHA-256 identifies bytes/code but cannot authenticate a replaceable manifest. Physical measurement and calibration procedures remain external unfinished work.
