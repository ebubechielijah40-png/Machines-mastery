# Controlled project experiment

Run Q-MEASURE-01 in the [full experiment protocol](../../20-quantum-computing/experiments/MEASUREMENT.md). It includes research questions, predictions, controls, independent/dependent variables, instrumentation, procedure, actual results, interpretation, alternative explanations, limitations and follow-up.

Before viewing output, derive P(1) for the phase sweep and predict equality probabilities for Bell versus a classical mixture in ZZ/XX. Then run the script, retain its JSON and compare numerical errors to expected floating-point effects. Explain why a simulator reproducing its own formal model is not independent evidence of quantum hardware behavior.

For an unexecuted extension, collect at least 30 independent pseudorandom-stream trials at 256 and 4096 shots and estimate frequency standard deviations. Predict a factor of four, but do not cherry-pick seeds or require every individual error to shrink. The [assessment](../../20-quantum-computing/assessments/EXERCISES.md), task 12, defines the deliverable; its observations are NOT YET OBSERVED.
