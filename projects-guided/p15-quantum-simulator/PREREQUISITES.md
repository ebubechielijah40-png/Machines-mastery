# Prerequisites and learning objectives

Use the detailed [seven-domain bridge](../../20-quantum-computing/STATEVECTOR_PREREQUISITE_BRIDGE.md). Required skills are fractions/square roots, Python lists and loops, binary indexing, complex conjugates, matrix-vector multiplication, probability normalization and conditioning. The [lesson](../../20-quantum-computing/10_STATEVECTOR_MEASUREMENT.md) introduces the exact math it uses; electronics and fabrication knowledge are deferred because no device is built.

Before coding, calculate `|(2+i)/3|²`, multiply H by |0>, list q1 amplitude pairs for n=3, and explain the difference between one observed specimen and many fresh preparations. If a task is missing, study its lesson section and retry; do not infer readiness from this checklist.

By the end of an evidenced build, you should derive a local gate update, prove norm preservation under an ideal unitary, trace a controlled gate through the array, distinguish common and relative phase, implement partial projection without destroying unrelated coherence, detect invalid states and identify the boundary between the mathematical model and a physical quantum machine. No learning outcome is claimed achieved until your independent attempt demonstrates it.
