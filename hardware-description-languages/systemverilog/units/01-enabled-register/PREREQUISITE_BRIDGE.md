# Foundations before RTL

Mathematics: [finite words](../../../../foundations/mathematics/finite-representations/README.md) and [state/Boolean logic](../../../../foundations/mathematics/state-and-logic/README.md). Physics and electronics: [electrical quantities and digital abstraction](../../../../foundations/physics/electrical-quantities/README.md), including power and why voltage interpretation is device-specific. Programming: the lesson explains its module, conditional and assignment syntax; no C semantics should be transferred blindly. Chemistry/materials: not required for Boolean simulation; semiconductor physics is a later gate for transistor implementation. Statistics: no probability or population inference. Systems: current/next state and event ordering are taught in the state bridge. None is assessed as learned.
