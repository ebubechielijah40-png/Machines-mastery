# Hints after a saved attempt

1–4. A response belongs to a particular operation, not the latest state. A key and
its effect must cross the same atomic boundary. Silence does not locate the commit.
5–8. Keep t as a fraction. A percentile is an order statistic of a specified union;
individual summaries discard counts and samples. Try one shard much larger than another.
9–11. Sum the three adjacent intervals. Two independent workers can complete at
most two12 ms services in12 ms before overhead; queuing adds no service capacity.
12–14. Use events to hold an admitted worker while offering more work. The process
fixture emits a phase marker before the coordinator kills its direct child.
15–16. Retained identity history is a premise of retry safety. A value seen later
can match multiple histories; add a durable operation receipt rather than guessing.
