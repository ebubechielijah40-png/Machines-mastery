# Sources: Model Serving and Production ML

## Primary References
1. **Sculley et al. (2015):** *Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems* (SE4ML / NeurIPS). Foundations of data validation, pipeline debt, and distribution monitoring.
2. **Yurdakul (2018):** *Statistical Properties of the Population Stability Index*. Formulation and thresholds for PSI as a covariate shift detector.
3. **Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville (2016):** *Deep Learning*, Chapter 4 (Numerical Computation) and Chapter 5 (Machine Learning Basics). Numerical stability in sigmoid/cross-entropy and SGD dynamics.
4. **Google SRE Book (2016):** *Service Level Objectives* and *Addressing Cascading Failures*. Foundations of latency percentiles (p50/p95/p99) and load shedding via rate limiting.
