# Exercises: finite information

1. Compute H for one certain outcome, eight equally likely outcomes, and probabilities [1/2,1/4,1/8,1/8].
2. Construct a joint table with independent but biased X,Y. Compute its marginals and show the direct mutual-information sum is zero.
3. Before running the experiment, predict the information at flip rates .5 and1. Explain why error rate and recoverable information differ.
4. A 32-byte output is repeated for every query. What can be concluded about empirical byte frequencies, uncertainty across query outputs, and cryptographic unpredictability? Keep these three questions separate.
5. Reconstruction: implement entropy and joint information from the equations. Check literal distributions, deterministic inversion and a separately written direct log-ratio oracle.
