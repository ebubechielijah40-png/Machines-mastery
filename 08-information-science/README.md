# Information Science

Curriculum phase: P08 — Information Science and Scientific Reasoning. Learning state: NOT STARTED.

Focus here on representation, context, metadata and provenance.

## Scope

data, information and knowledge; fact and claim; evidence; context; metadata; provenance; measurement; uncertainty; source criticism; scientific method; experimental design; sampling; correlation and causation; reproducibility and replication.

## Entry and exit

Use the unit-level gates in [PREREQUISITE_GRAPH.md](../PREREQUISITE_GRAPH.md). Target evidence: Trace a claim back to collection and measurement; delimit what the evidence supports.
