# Reverse Engineering Philosophy

[EXPLANATION] Original investigation method, not a claim about uninspected devices.

An observed result may have several internal explanations. Keep multiple models until a test distinguishes them. A functional replica demonstrates a behavior contract, not knowledge of original authorship or identical internals.

Practice: Write two mechanisms that could produce the same light output and identify one observation that separates them.

Record the boundary, inputs, current state, predicted output, observation method, assumptions and a result that would force revision. When no distinguishing experiment is available, report the equivalence class of surviving models. No observation or mastery is claimed by this method page.
