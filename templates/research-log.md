# Research Log Template

Record date, investigator, question, scope and stopping condition.

## Search and selection

Record actual queries, opened primary sources, inclusion/exclusion reasons, source identities/revisions, failed retrievals and unresolved contradictions. Search snippets do not count as inspected evidence.

## Evidence and method

Link claim records, source locators, protocols and actual raw data. Distinguish literature claims, explanations, hypotheses and observations. Record tools/commands actually used and their outputs; keep planned commands separate.

## Findings and decisions

State supported conclusions, alternatives, sources of error and limitations. For reproduction, compare against a specified original result and tolerance; for replication, define what changes in data or setup. Do not invent agreement.

## Follow-up

Identify the smallest action that resolves an important uncertainty. Update PROGRESS only from actual work and learner evidence.
