# Experiment Protocol Template

## Identity and question

Assign an E- identifier, project, phase and one bounded question. Register its path and source IDs before execution.

## Background

Cite inspected source locators and state what remains unverified.

## Hypothesis

Write a falsifiable prediction and the model assumptions it depends on.

## Variables and controls

Identify manipulated variables, measured outcomes, controlled conditions, baseline and potential confounders.

## Setup

Record actual platform, tool versions, input/data provenance, seeds, source/code hashes and local safety boundaries. Until chosen, mark them unverified.

## Procedure and measurements

Specify steps, measurement units, repeats, raw-output locations, expected failure behavior and stopping conditions before running. Do not describe proposed commands as executed.

## Expected result

Prediction only; explain why it follows from the model.

## Observed result

NOT YET OBSERVED.

## Analysis and alternative explanations

Pending execution. Compare actual observations to predictions; consider competing mechanisms and confounding.

## Sources of error

Identify measurement limitations, selection effects, noise and implementation mistakes. Separate anticipated errors from observed errors.

## Conclusion and follow-up

No empirical conclusion before execution. State what the evidence supports, what it cannot distinguish and the next discriminating test.
