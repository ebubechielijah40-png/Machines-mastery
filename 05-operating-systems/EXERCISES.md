# OS exercises — preserve unaided attempts

1. Distinguish executable file, process, thread and ready queue with concrete state.
2. Compute FCFS response/turnaround/wait for A(0,5),B(1,2),C(2,1).
3. Reconstruct the quantum2 RR trace and explain the t=2 boundary order.
4. A job arrives at3 after idle time and runs4 ticks alone. With switch cost100,
   what should this model charge, and why does a quantum expiry not imply a switch?
5. Derive useful fraction q/(q+c). List premises and a real factor absent from c.
6. For jobs of lengths2 and7 arriving together, compare both completion sums.
7. With16-byte pages, translate VA0xa7 if VPN10 maps frame20. Give all fields.
8. Explain why a TLB hit must still honor read-only permission after a write request.
9. A cache contains VPN2→7 and the page table changes to→9. Construct the stale result.
10. Why can two process contexts both use VA0 while resolving different frames?
11. Predict three reads from original, duplicate and separate-open descriptors.
12. A reader hangs after the producer exits. Design a local descriptor-lifetime
    hypothesis that does not blame an absent newline or a slow CPU.
13. Debug: why can waiting for a child before consuming its piped output deadlock?
14. Experiment: run the declared sweep and host observations. Separate exact
    synthetic model results from actual host observations and their limits.
15. Reconstruct the scheduler and translator from a blank file. Transfer the
    address geometry to10-bit VA and32-byte pages, defining a two-level split.
    Deliver an independent oracle, protection/stale-cache failures and limitations.
