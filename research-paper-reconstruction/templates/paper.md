# Paper reconstruction contract

Create `papers/<verified-slug>/` only after intake, with these substantive files:

- `00_SOURCE.md`
- `01_CONTEXT.md`
- `02_RESEARCH_PROBLEM.md`
- `03_PRIOR_WORK.md`
- `04_CORE_IDEA.md`
- `05_METHOD.md`
- `06_SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md`
- `07_ALGORITHMS.md`
- `08_EXPERIMENTS.md`
- `09_RESULTS.md`
- `10_EVIDENCE.md`
- `11_LIMITATIONS.md`
- `12_CRITIQUE.md`
- `13_REPRODUCTION.md`
- `14_CONNECTIONS.md`
- `15_QUESTIONS.md`

Explain prior world and failed approaches; precise question; non-obvious insight; inputs/outputs/stages/data structures/architecture/assumptions; every major design choice and alternative; experiment variables, controls, baselines and metrics; actual results versus unsupported claims; uncertainty, failure cases and external validity. Reproduction specifies code/data/environment/dependencies/procedure/expected outputs separately from observed results. Connect earlier curriculum and later technology with evidence. Questions must include a falsifying experiment, strongest assumption and competing explanation. Distinguish source claim, derivation and interpretation with exact locators. Do not turn the paper into an ordinary summary or redistribute it.
