# Seminar sequence

Select one domain with readable original sources and explicit prerequisites. Verify metadata, then trace one central claim back through experiment/proof, method and assumptions. Reconstruct a mechanism with original examples. Design an independent challenge before reproducing. Pin code/data revisions, state access failures, and validate the exemplar before adding the next paper. Do not claim a ten-paper release from ten bibliographic links. Candidate selection and source acquisition are the next unfinished paper steps.
