# Hints

1–7. Count the fixed dopant charge as well as mobile charge. A cubic-centimeter
conversion involves the cube of length; mobility involves its square. Avoid
subtracting nearly equal roots; obtain the minority through the product law.
Charge times signed flux determines conventional-current direction.

8–14. Integrate local potential, then inspect the endpoint inequality. A circuit
load line supplies a second equation. Multiply current balance by output voltage
for energy. In Euler, expand (V+ΔV)² before comparing the stored-energy change with
old-state power. Preserve discrepancies rather than clipping.

15–19. Series paths require both switches, parallel paths require at least one.
The carry counts an extra power of2. Draw separate current and next capture edges;
hold and setup refer to different events. Extra delay affects both inequalities.

20–22. An analog sampling node is not an ideal Boolean variable. A coherent bus
needs a coherent transfer contract, not only per-bit probability reduction. Start
your reconstruction with assumptions and observable acceptance criteria.
