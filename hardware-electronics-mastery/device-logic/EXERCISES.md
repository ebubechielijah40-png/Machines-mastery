# Reconstruct before using abstractions

1. Explain why a hole is not a moving proton and why an n-type bulk sample can
   remain electrically neutral.
2. Convert10^16 cm^-3 to m^-3 and1000 cm²/(V·s) to SI mobility.
3. Derive the carrier quadratic and the minority-carrier expression.
4. Explain the cancellation failure for large negative dopant imbalance and the
   stable majority/minority construction.
5. With ni=10^16 m^-3, ND=10^20 m^-3, NA=0, estimate n,p and their product.
6. Give a compensation example with large total impurity density but no net
   imbalance. Which real transport effect does the fixed-mobility model omit?
7. Derive drift-current signs and conductivity units from signed charge flux.
8. Integrate the long-channel charge law. State exactly where its nonnegative-
   charge assumption ceases to hold.
9. Compute the three current cases in the lesson. Why is extrapolating the
   triode parabola past its allowed range wrong?
10. Explain why MOSFET saturation does not mean zero source-drain resistance.
11. Derive the resistor-load KCL bracket and show why its root is unique for the
    declared model. Check cutoff and the high-input quadratic independently.
12. Account for supply/resistor/device/capacitor power without omitting a port.
13. Derive the Euler energy residual and compare it to the retained observation.
14. Predict and then execute a doubled-load-capacitance experiment with a
    correspondingly controlled integration step.
15. Derive NAND and NOR from pull-up/pull-down connectivity. Exhibit an incorrect
    complementary network with both rails conducting or neither connected.
16. Derive the full-adder truth table and prove how a ripple chain represents
    a+b=out+2^width·carry. Use an independent integer oracle.
17. Distinguish contamination, propagation, setup, hold and clock-to-Q delays.
18. Rebuild both timing inequalities from launch/current-capture/next-capture
    events, declaring the sign of skew and meaning of uncertainty.
19. Reproduce the hold counterexample; propose a minimum-delay repair and recheck
    its setup consequence.
20. Give an asynchronous-input scenario where a perfect0/1 RTL simulation supplies
    no evidence about metastability failure probability.
21. Explain why independent two-flop synchronizers on every data bit do not by
    themselves preserve a multi-bit value. State a protocol-level alternative.
22. Rebuild the carrier, circuit and timing model from empty files. Include an
    input rejection, a distinct numerical oracle, a failed experiment and source
    scopes. Separate executed synthetic output from a future physical protocol.
