# Gaps retained honestly

The whole book remains PARTIAL. This companion covers lexical binding and
closures through a small supplied AST; it does not implement a complete scanner,
parser, class/object system, bytecode virtual machine, garbage collector, native
interoperability or full Lox language. Only the selected author scopes in the
source record were inspected in this batch. Other chapter mappings and their
experiments must be developed from actual originals before they can be completed.
The original web source is excluded; exact local-original replay is therefore
unavailable from this source-only companion archive.
