## First-unit decision
[EXPLANATION] Select **representation, physical signals and message boundaries**, grounded in Chapter 1, PDF 6–10. It requires no programming, electronics, binary arithmetic or previously established machine model. It gives the later word “binary” an operational question: what are the distinguishable observations, and how are their meanings and boundaries agreed? Starting with gates would leave that representational contract implicit. The dependency choice happens to select Chapter 1; its number is not the reason.
