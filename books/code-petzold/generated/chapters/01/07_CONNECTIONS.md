# Connections and pending dependencies

[EXPLANATION] This unit operationalizes the representation → transmission → interpretation portion of the organizing model in LEARNING_SYSTEM. The input statement can be false even when transmission and decoding are correct. Integrity of representation and truth of a claim are different questions.

The required prior practice is U00: identify source, distinguish a source-backed premise from an original derivation, and preserve predictions before observation. No prior learner mastery is assumed.

Forward connection to U02/J-01: a gate component needs an agreed input/output interpretation. This unit establishes why an interface contract matters, but does not explain voltage levels or how a gate works.

Forward connection to U03: the unfinished buffer demonstrates a need for retained state. It is not evidence that the learner understands flip-flops or physical memory.

Forward connection to U10: a stream may need message boundaries in addition to a symbol encoding. The A/ET collision supplies a small counterexample, not a TCP protocol explanation.

Forward connection to U12/U15: a correctly decoded record is not automatically a trustworthy observation. To investigate truth or provenance, introduce separate evidence and threat models in those later units.

Chapter 2 and combinatorial capacity remain unprocessed. These connections are bounded design consequences, not permission to skip future prerequisites.
