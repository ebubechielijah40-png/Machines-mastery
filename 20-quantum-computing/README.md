# Quantum computing and quantum machines

This track teaches quantum computing from the mathematical and physical ideas needed to understand what a quantum computer is actually doing, then connects circuits to hardware constraints and cryptographic implications.

Normal order:

1. complex numbers and probability
2. vectors, matrices, inner products, tensor products
3. classical information vs quantum state
4. qubits, amplitudes, phase, Bloch-sphere intuition
5. unitary gates
6. multi-qubit state and entanglement
7. measurement
8. circuit composition
9. algorithms and query complexity
10. noise, decoherence, error correction
11. hardware modalities/control stack
12. quantum networking/cryptography concepts
13. what quantum computers can and cannot accelerate
