# Hints

1–2. Dagger conjugates i to−i. Use U=(1/√2)[[1,−i],[1,i]] before a Z measurement.
3. For a>0 group the quadratic form into a squared modulus and one residual;
   handle a0 separately. Positive diagonal entries alone are insufficient.
4–5. Z changes the sign of each off-diagonal. Relaxation transfers population
   from the lower-right entry to the upper-left while attenuating coherence.
6–7. A tensor cube has2³ terms. Complementing all three bits preserves parity.
8. Count weights0,1,2,3, then compare the polynomial to p by factorization.
9–10. All codewords have syndrome00. A coherent phase error need not flip a bit.
11. Two logical X operations cancel, so count exactly one of the two error events.
12. A syndrome outcome has its own normalized state and probability; never add
   normalized branch amplitudes as if the measurement had not occurred.
