# Quantum limits and reality checks

- Exact statevector simulation scales exponentially in qubit count.
- A superposition does not let you read all branches.
- Entanglement does not permit controllable faster-than-light communication.
- Algorithmic speedup is problem-specific and must include oracle/input/output assumptions.
- Near-term hardware is noisy and limited; ideal circuit success does not predict hardware success without a noise/resource model.
- Fault-tolerant logical qubits require many physical resources; raw qubit count is not a complete capacity metric.
- Quantum algorithms do not “break encryption” in general; impacts depend on the primitive.
