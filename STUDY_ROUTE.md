# Dependency-driven study route

This route is designed for breadth **without** becoming a collection of disconnected tutorials.

## Route A — machine foundations

1. Number representation and Boolean reasoning
2. Electricity needed for switching
3. Transistor-as-switch model
4. Gates → combinational logic → state
5. CPU datapath/control and memory
6. RISC-V/x86/ARM assembly bridges
7. C and systems programming
8. Operating systems
9. Networking
10. Databases/data systems
11. Security/cryptography/privacy
12. Distributed and production systems

## Route B — computation and intelligence

1. Discrete math and algorithms
2. Probability/statistics
3. Linear algebra/calculus just-in-time
4. Data science
5. Machine learning
6. AI systems and evaluation
7. Security/reliability of models

## Route C — physical machines

1. Units, force, energy, power
2. Electricity/electronics
3. Sensors/actuators
4. Embedded software
5. Feedback/control
6. Robotics and machine teardown/reconstruction

## Route D — life as an information-bearing physical system

1. Chemistry/materials bridge
2. Cell and molecular biology
3. Genetics and information flow
4. Sequence algorithms
5. Genomics and statistics
6. Evolution and phylogenetics
7. Systems biology
8. Structural/computational biology
9. Synthetic biology as design theory (safe, non-procedural)
10. Reproducible biological-data projects

## Route E — quantum machines

1. Complex numbers and probability
2. Vectors/matrices/tensor products
3. Classical bits vs qubits
4. Single-qubit gates and phase
5. Multi-qubit systems and entanglement
6. Measurement and circuits
7. Algorithms and speedup claims
8. Noise/error correction
9. Quantum hardware modalities
10. Cryptographic implications and limits

Routes interleave. Do not finish an entire math curriculum before touching systems; use internal prerequisite bridges just in time.

## Cross-cutting security rule
Security and cryptography are not postponed until one late phase. At every system boundary identify assets, trust, authentication/authorization, integrity, privacy, availability, and recovery. The dedicated cryptography track supplies mechanisms when needed.
